The Floating-Point Problem

Did you know standard calculators get simple math wrong? Because they rely on floating-point arithmetic, they often take shortcuts that result in tiny, invisible inaccuracies (like 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004).

In complex or chained equations, these tiny errors can quickly snowball into significant mistakes.
